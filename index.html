Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find instant answers regarding eligibility, required documents, and DBT verification

Who is eligible for the National Fellowship for ST Students?

Any Scheduled Tribe student who has secured regular admission in M.Phil/Ph.D courses in universities recognized by UGC, with an aggregate annual parental income not exceeding ₹6,00,000.

What is DigiLocker digital token verification?

Instead of manually submitting physical stamped papers, the portal securely pulls your verified Caste and Income Certificate directly from state issuing authorities via DigiLocker cryptographic APIs.

What file formats are accepted for document uploads?

The portal strictly accepts document uploads in PDF and JPG/JPEG format only to maintain integrity and prevent security issues.

How can I resolve DBT payment failure?

DBT transfers require an active bank account linked to your Aadhaar card on the NPCI server. Visit your home bank branch to request 'NPCI Aadhaar Mapping'.
